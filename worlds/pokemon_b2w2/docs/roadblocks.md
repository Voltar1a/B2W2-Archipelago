# Roadblocks

## Gym badge gates - Mostly vanilla-adjacent
- Basic Badge: Unblocks Hiker Jerome on Route 20 (East).
- Toxic Badge: Unblocks the Virbank Harbor ferry to Castelia City.
- Insect Badge: Unblocks Colress and the Crustle on Route 4.
- Bolt Badge: Unblocks Driftveil Drawbridge (via Route 5).
- Quake Badge: Unblocks Chargestone Cave Floor 2.
- Jet Badge: Unblocks Mistralton flights to Lentimas Town.
- Legend Badge: Unblocks the Marine Tube between Undella Town and Humilau City.
- All 8 Badges: Unblocks Victory Road at the badge check gates on Route 23.

## Field moves and items
- Card Key: Unblocks the Team Plasma grunts at the Route 22 entrance to Giant Chasm.
- Plasma Card: Allows boarding the Plasma Frigate in Giant Chasm.
- DNA Splicers: Unblocks the Team Plasma grunts at the Giant Chasm exit to Route 23.
- Colress MCHN: Clears Crustle in Seaside Cave to reach Route 21/Humilau City.
- HMs and Flash: Should hopefully be obvious.

- Seasons and days: `Spring`/`Summer`/`Autumn`/`Winter` and `Sunday`–`Saturday` unlock seasonal/day-specific checks and can be swapped in the APtransceiver once acquired.
