# Pokémon Black 2 and White 2

## What is this game?

Pokémon Black 2 and White 2 are the sequels to the fifth generation Pokémon games set two years later in the Unova region.
Explore new areas and gym challenges across Unova, face off against Neo Team Plasma, and defeat Iris at the Pokémon League.
