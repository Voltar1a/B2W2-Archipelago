# Changes and Roadblocks

Overview of the main gameplay, progression, and QoL changes in this mod compared to vanilla.

## Roadblocks and progression gates

### Gym badge gates
- **Basic Badge**: Unblocks Hiker Jerome midway through Route 20.
- **Toxic Badge**: Unblocks the Virbank Harbor ferry to Castelia City.
- **Insect Badge**: Unblocks Colress and the Crustle on Route 4.
- **Bolt Badge**: Unblocks Driftveil Drawbridge (via Route 5) by making Motorcyclist Charles battleable.
- **Quake Badge**: Unblocks Bianca and the Workers halfway through Chargestone Cave.
- **Jet Badge**: Unblocks Mistralton flights to Lentimas Town (and the Nature Preserve with the Permit).
- **Legend Badge**: Unblocks the Marine Tube between Undella Town and Humilau City.
- **All 8 badges**: Unblocks Victory Road at the badge check gates on Route 23.

### Key items and story gates
- **Plasma Card**: Unblocks the Team Plasma Grunts at the Route 22 entrance to Giant Chasm.
- **DNA Splicers**: Unblocks the Team Plasma Grunts at the Giant Chasm exit to Route 23.
- **Colress MCHN**: Clears Crustle in Seaside Cave to reach Route 21 and allows boarding the Route 21 Plasma Frigate.
- **Permit**: Unlocks flights from Mistralton City to the Nature Preserve (also needs Jet Badge).
- **Magma Stone / Lunar Wing**: Gate the Heatran (Reversal Mountain) and Cresselia (Marvelous Bridge) legendary encounters.

### Field moves, seasons and days
- **HMs and Flash**: Cut, Fly, Surf, Strength, Waterfall, Dive, and Flash.
- **Seasons and days**: Spring/Summer/Autumn/Winter and Sunday-Saturday are shuffled items that unlock seasonal and day-specific checks. You start with one random season and one random day and can swap between any unlocked ones in the APtransceiver (some are needed for certain checks).

## APtransceiver
The Xtransceiver (on START button) is replaced with the APtransceiver, giving access to:
- **PC**: Access Pokémon storage from anywhere.
- **Field moves**: Teleport, Dig, Fly (to visited towns once you have HM02 Fly), and Flash (once you have TM70 Flash).
- **Seasons and days**: Switch to any season or day of the week you have unlocked.
- **Check log & mdifiers**: View recent received items and toggle in-game QoL modifiers (Auto-Run, Bike Everywhere, Blind Trainers, Guaranteed Catch, Guaranteed Escape, Fast Fishing, Hold A Turbo, Catch Rate / EXP multipliers, and Check Log Filter).

## Missables & Relaxed Check Requirements
- **Autocollected missables**:
  - Clyde's gifts and Gym trainers (in trainersanity) autocollect when you defeat the corresponding Gym Leader if you skipped them.
  - Missable Opelucid City gifts autocollect after Drayden's Kyurem talk.
  - Missable trainers/items on the Route 21 and Giant Chasm Plasma Frigates autocollect upon finishing their respective Frigate sequence if skipped.
- **Simplified NPC gift requirements**: Restrictive species, stat, held-item, IRL time-of-day, and external-app requirements have been removed so checks can't softlock (e.g. Abundant Shrine Elder no longer needs Dream Radar, Village Bridge Fisherman no longer needs the daily quest, Marine Tube no longer needs a Poison Barb, Accumula Town size/species checks and Humilau Nurse's Luvdisc check are bypassed, Mistralton Pilot's type check and Driftveil Luxury Suites' TM move check are bypassed, and time-of-day checks are always active). These may be changed later if I can think of how to add their requirements nicely.
- **Frigate changes**: All four Plasma Frigate appearances are isolated from each other so progressing or clearing one does not break or lock out checks on the others.

## Evolutions & Story Speedups
- **Solo and anytime-friendly evolutions**:
  - Trade evolutions are converted to level-up (Lv. 37) or held-item level up evolutions (day or night) so every evolution is obtainable without trading.
  - Single branch time-of-day evolutions (Budew, Riolu, Chingling, Happiny, Gligar, Sneasel) evolve at any time of day; Eevee keeps Day/Night so Espeon and Umbreon both remain obtainable.
  - With randomised level up movesets enabled, moves required for move-based evolutions (e.g. Ancient Power, Rollout, Double Hit, Mimic) are preserved in their learnsets so those Pokémon can still evolve.
- **Optional story skips** (configured in YAML): Skip intro, Living Room Starter, Skip Driftveil PWT tournament, Skip Join Avenue, Skip Route 19 Catching Tutorial, Skip mandatory Pokéstar Studios sequence, and Skip 3D Cinematic Movies.
